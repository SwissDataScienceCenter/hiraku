# Guided research program

Work step by step and keep `task.md` and experiment records current.

Pause for explicit human approval at these checkpoints:

1. after drafting the task scope, goal, evaluation protocol, and implementation plan;
2. after defining an experiment and implementing it, before committing or binding its source;
3. before launching any run; and
4. before finalizing findings, conclusions, and decisions.

Do not commit changes, launch runs, change an approved evaluation protocol, or merge into a protected branch without explicit approval. Surface the relevant files and proposed commands at every checkpoint. Treat this program as workflow guidance, not as a security boundary.
