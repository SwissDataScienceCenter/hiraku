The UCI record states that patient names and social security numbers were removed and replaced with
dummy values. This example uses only the processed 14-variable Cleveland file.

## Intended use

This is an educational workflow and software-integration demonstration. It is not a diagnostic,
clinical, or medical decision-support system, and its metrics must not be interpreted as evidence
of clinical validity.
